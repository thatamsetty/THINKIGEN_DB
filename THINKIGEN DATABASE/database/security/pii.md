# PII and Sensitive Data

Student, guardian, teacher/employee, contact, academic and financial information is sensitive.

Rules:
- collect only approved fields;
- minimize exposure;
- do not log secrets or unnecessary PII;
- protect exports;
- restrict reports by authorization scope;
- protect lower environments from production PII;
- retain required history;
- do not delete protected guardian/student history merely because a relationship changes.

Guardian records may be associated with multiple students.
